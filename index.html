✿ how we craft

From skein to story

Every Hoshii piece follows the same slow, three-step ritual — nothing rushed, nothing machine-made.

Choose the yarn

We hand-pick soft, breathable cotton and blends in dreamy pastel shades for every piece.

Stitch by hand

Each item is hooked loop by loop, at a real crocheter's pace — never a machine in sight.

Finish with love

We weave in every end, press each piece, and tuck it in tissue before it heads your way.
